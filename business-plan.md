## Positioning

**Who:** Nicolae Cojuhari - finance-fluent full-stack software engineer, 80% fintech-focused, 20% broader business IT solutions.

**For whom (two audiences now):**
1. **Fintech/finance-adjacent employers** in Vienna and DACH (banks, neobanks, payment processors, insurtechs) evaluating him for full-time engineering roles.
2. Small and medium businesses in Vienna (and remotely across DACH) that are:
   - Still using Excel/spreadsheets for things that should be apps
   - Doing repetitive manual work that should be automated
   - Selling online and need a proper Shopify setup

**One-sentence pitch:**
> "Finance-fluent software engineer - I build secure, AI-enabled software for fintech and business operations. Open to full-time roles, and taking select consulting projects."

**Not:** A developer for hire by the hour. Not a generalist full-stack dev. A finance-fluent engineer who happens to also take select projects.

## Services + Pricing

| Service | What it is | Price range |
|---|---|---|
| **Business Website** | Fast, modern, SEO-ready site for SMEs | €1,500 – €3,000 |
| **Ecommerce (Shopify)** | Full Shopify setup, custom theme, payments | €2,500 – €6,000 |
| **Excel Killer App** | Replace spreadsheets with a proper web tool (inventory, client mgmt, finance, HR) | €2,500 – €8,000 |
| **Process Automation** | Eliminate manual workflows (forms, emails, reports, integrations) | €1,500 – €4,000 |
| **CRO / Redesign** | Improve existing site to convert better | €1,000 – €2,500 |
| **Monthly Retainer** | Ongoing support, updates, hosting mgmt | €300 – €800/mo |

**The Excel Killer is the highest-leverage offer.** Every Vienna SME - Handwerker, clinics, law firms, restaurants, retailers - is running something critical in Excel. They don't know what's possible or what it costs. Walk in with "I'll replace your spreadsheet with a tool your whole team can use on any device" - that's a €3-6k project with massive perceived value and zero competition from cheap freelancers on Fiverr.

## Additional angles

- **AI integrations** - hot demand, premium pricing, and directly reinforces the fintech-employer story. Add AI features to existing tools (chatbots, document processing, automation). Most SMEs are asking about AI but don't know where to start - and every AI feature shipped is also an interview talking point.
- **Security-conscious development** - same logic: OWASP-aware, auth/secrets-conscious builds are a client trust signal AND an employer credibility signal. Don't build two versions of this skill - one competency, two audiences.
- **White-label / agency subcontracting** - be the dev behind Vienna agencies. Lower priority now than before the job-search pivot; useful for cash flow, not the main focus.
- **Handwerker-Netz crossover** - clients from the handyman business who need websites or tools. Warm leads, trust already built. Fine to keep opportunistically, don't actively chase.

2. About/Positioning - finance degree, 15 years in consumer credit, security overlay, applied AI - the core narrative
3. Case studies - Ideal Credit loan-tracking (anchor, 80%), Trackr + AI feature once shipped (80%), one breadth piece (20%)
4. Services - consulting offer menu, now secondary to the case studies, not the hero

7. Contact - recruiter-friendly (CV download, LinkedIn, email) leading; WhatsApp/form for consulting leads secondary
